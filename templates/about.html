About FoodBridge

Building a smarter, kinder food ecosystem for rural India.

FoodBridge was created to solve a dual crisis: rising food waste and persistent hunger in underserved villages. Surplus food from restaurants, hotels, temples, weddings, and households often gets discarded due to poor logistics and lack of matching networks.

Our platform connects donors with nearby NGOs, volunteers, and recipients using a simple, mobile-first interface. We combine digital records, location intelligence, and safety checks to make food redistribution efficient, traceable, and reliable.

Our Mission

  • Reduce food waste in rural and semi-urban communities.
  • Improve access to nutritious meals for vulnerable families.
  • Empower local volunteers and civil society groups.
